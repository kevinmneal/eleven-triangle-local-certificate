# Attribution and AI assistance

The eleven-triangle construction is attributed to Timo Berthold, Dominik
Kamp, Gioni Mexi, Sebastian Pokutta and Imre Pólik, through their solution
repository and Erich Friedman's packing catalogue. This repository does not
claim discovery of that packing.

Kevin M. Neal directed the subsequent research, selected questions and scope,
and curated this release. OpenAI Codex and ChatGPT were used extensively for
mathematical derivation, implementation, experiment orchestration, writing and
internal review. The generated arguments and proof data are presented with
their executable checks and explicit trust boundary, not on the authority of
an AI response.

The included mathematical and implementation audits were conducted during
that AI-assisted workflow. They inspect different aspects of the result but
share the same consumer and arithmetic components. They are not independent
external replication or human peer review. No endorsement by the construction's
authors or by OpenAI is asserted.
